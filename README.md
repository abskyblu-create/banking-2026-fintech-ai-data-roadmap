# Banking 2026  Fintech, AI & Data Transformation

## Overview
**Banking 2026** is a visual industry briefing exploring how **AI, fintech platforms, and data ecosystems** are reshaping global financial services.

This project presents market trends, industry statistics, and strategic shifts affecting banking, payments, and fintech innovation worldwide.

The goal is to provide a concise, visually engaging, and data-informed perspective on where financial services are heading.

---

## Key Themes Covered

- Growth of global digital and non-cash payments
- Fintech revenue expansion and platform ecosystems
- AI adoption across banking operations
- Data as a competitive advantage
- Rise of hybrid financial infrastructure
- Emerging decentralized finance and tokenized asset ecosystems
- Strategic priorities for banks through 2026

## Data Sources

Statistics and insights are compiled from publicly available industry reports and analyses, including:

- BCG Global Fintech Reports
- McKinsey Financial Services Insights
- Capgemini World Payments Report
- Fortune Business Insights
- Statista industry outlooks
- Industry market intelligence platforms

Figures represent **directional market estimates and forecasts** intended to illustrate industry trends rather than precise predictions.

## Why This Matters

Banking is moving toward a model that combines:

- Trust of traditional institutions
- Speed of fintech innovation
- Intelligence of AI systems
- Power of data platforms

Financial leaders will be institutions that integrate these capabilities responsibly and effectively.
